The reality at the top

Health cannot be delegated.

Senior leaders carry long days, regular travel, broken sleep and the weight of decisions that affect many people.

Over years load accumulates as a stiffness, a sore neck or shoulder, fading energy and training that stops every time something flares.

  • Long haul flights, client visits and hotel rooms make routines hard to hold
  • Pain and stress tend to rise together, each feeding the other
  • Exercise is often sacrificed when the diary fills
  • Small problems are managed quietly until they become big ones

Pain and burnout rise together: Vitrue Health workforce data, reported by REBA, March 2026.

The common cycle

Plenty of information. little action.

The annual health check

A thorough assessment and a detailed report, then you are largely on your own until next year.

The personal trainer

Motivating and convenient, but rarely equipped to work around pain, injury or fatigue. 

The physiotherapist

Excellent when something has gone wrong, but usually seen only once it already has.

The app and the wearable

Plenty of data, but they do not know you, your history or what your life actually looks like.

What is missing is one expert who knows you, joins it all up, and stays with you.

How it works

The first six months.

Month 1

Discovery

An in depth first session with David, in clinic, online, or wherever suits you. This sets the foundation for everything that follows.

  • Full history, including injuries, pain, lifestyle, goals, training background...
  • Agreeing one concrete goal and what success will look and feel like
  • Movement, strength and capacity assessment, and conditioning markers
  • Review of wearable data on sleep, heart rate variability and recovery
  • A clear explanation of findings, so you understand your own body and the plan
Day one

Handover

You leave the first session with your programme already live on your phone, with David's own unique exercise videos, the reasoning behind each element and audio guidance, so the direction is clear from the outset.

Months 2 to 5

In your corner

The core of the programme, refreshed and progressed every month, with David alongside you between sessions.

  • Bespoke programming from David, refreshed monthly in blocks
  • A weekly plan built around your diary, with travel and hotel room versions
  • A direct line to David for questions, flare ups and changes of plan
  • Short check ins by voice or text that land in David's clinical notes
  • Regular one to one sessions, in person or by video
  • Priority clinical review if pain or injury appears
Month 6

Reassess and renew

A full reassessment against your starting point, a clear picture of what changed and why, and a new goal so that the progress continues.

Between sessions

Your programme lives on your phone with David's voice.

Athena Online carries your plan wherever you are, in the Ergon Executive design.

Sessions arrive in the right version for the day, whether in the gym at home, or twenty minutes in a hotel room. Short check ins on pain, sleep and energy go straight to David, so the plan keeps up with your life rather than the other way round.

Illustrative example

Sarah, a managing director who travels most weeks.

Sarah is a composite rather than a real client. In her late forties, she sleeps poorly in travel weeks and has managed a sore lower back for years by stopping exercise whenever it flares.

Week 1

Discovery session with David. Her back pain is explained in plain language, which removes much of the fear around it, and her goal is set: a week long family trek next summer, carrying her own pack.

Week 1

She leaves with three sessions a week in the app, including a 20 minute hotel room version, and a short daily mobility routine for long flights.

Weeks 3 to 6

Her wearable shows poor recovery in travel weeks, so heavier strength work moves to home weeks and recovery work to travel weeks.

Week 9

A flare after a long drive. She messages David, receives a recovery plan the same day and keeps training, which is the first time in years she has not stopped altogether.

Month 6

Reassessment shows clear gains in strength and loaded carrying, and she reports fewer flares. A loaded pack walk is added as the next milestone towards the trek.
